FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Do you take reservations for large groups? +
Yes, we accommodate group reservations! For parties larger than 8 guests, please reach out to us via our phone line or contact form at least 24 hours in advance.
Are there vegetarian and vegan options available? +
Absolutely. We offer craft vegetarian burgers, fresh salads, and custom pasta dishes tailored to dietary preferences.
Is parking available on site? +
Yes, we offer secure baseline parking with dedicated security attendants for all our guests in Westlands.
Do you host private events or corporate parties? +
Yes! Our venue is available for full or partial buyouts for private birthdays, corporate mixers, and special occasions.
